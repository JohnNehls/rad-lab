To improve the project and its documentation, consider these
enhancements:

* Features
- *SAR*
  - Comment that it should limit the useful SAR Aperture length per eqn 8.11

- *Monopulse Study*: LFM should introduce phase defference between reciver array positions and introduce errors into monopulse -- May have looked into this already!

- *Search radar, section 10 (range profiles, scanning, PPI):* follows
  section 9 (clutter and MTI).  Range profiles get their full introduction
  here rather than as a side figure in 9_2.
  - 10_0 Range profiles and non-coherent integration: single pulse vs. N
    pulses; check the integration gain against the theory in
    =detection.py= (as 6_1 does for Pd).  The profile is the pulse-summed
    power of the range-time matrix; with =window="none"= this equals the
    RDM power summed over Doppler (Parseval), so a small helper may be
    enough.
  - 10_1 MTI on range profiles: with no Doppler FFT, the canceller is the
    only clutter filter.  Quick test (target 120 m/s, 3 m/s clutter
    spread, 9_2 radar): target rose from ~4 dB above the clutter
    background to ~13-14 dB with order-1/2 MTI.  This is the motivation
    that 9_2's RDMs lack.
  - 10_2+ Beam scanning and PPI display: range profile per azimuth step,
    antenna-pattern weighting from =uniform_linear_arrays=, clutter and
    MTI on the display.  Simulate a few pulses per beam position, not a
    full RDM, to keep the apps regression fast.
  - Later: plot extraction and tracking (alpha-beta, then Kalman with
    gating) on detections across scans.

- *GPU Support:* For large data cubes, consider optional
  [[https://cupy.dev/][CuPy]] support for GPU-accelerated FFTs.
